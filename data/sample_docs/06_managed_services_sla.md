# NimbusEdge Technologies — Managed Cloud Services: Support Model and SLAs

**Document type:** Service level section for managed services proposals (v4.0, 2026)

## Support Coverage
24x7x365 support through a dedicated service desk, available by phone, email and our ticketing portal (ServiceNow).

## Incident Priority and Response Times
| Priority | Description | Response time | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 – Critical | Production down or major business impact | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 – High | Major function degraded | 30 minutes | 8 hours |
| P3 – Medium | Minor impact, workaround available | 4 hours | 3 business days |
| P4 – Low | Service requests and queries | 1 business day | 5 business days |

## Availability Commitment
We commit to 99.95% monthly availability for the infrastructure we manage. If availability falls below that, service credits apply: 5% of the monthly fee for each 0.1% below target, capped at 20%.

## Escalation Matrix
- Level 1: Service desk engineer
- Level 2: Cloud operations lead (after 30 minutes on a P1)
- Level 3: Service delivery manager (after 1 hour on a P1)
- Level 4: Head of Managed Services (after 2 hours on a P1)

## Reporting
- Monthly service report covering SLA performance, incidents, changes and cloud cost.
- Quarterly business review (QBR) with the client's leadership.

## Team Structure
Each managed client gets a named Service Delivery Manager and a Technical Account Manager.
