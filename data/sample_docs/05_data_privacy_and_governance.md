# NimbusEdge Technologies — Data Privacy and Governance

**Document type:** Privacy and compliance response pack (approved by DPO, v2.1, February 2026)

## Regulatory Compliance
- We comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
- For healthcare clients with US operations, we support HIPAA-aligned controls and sign Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) when required.
- We follow RBI guidelines on IT outsourcing for banking clients in India.

## Data Protection Officer
NimbusEdge has an appointed Data Protection Officer (DPO), reachable at a dedicated privacy mailbox listed in each contract.

## Data Residency
Client data stays in the region the client chooses. By default, data for Indian clients is stored only in Indian cloud regions. We do not move client data across borders without written approval.

## Data Retention and Deletion
- Client data is retained only for the period defined in the contract.
- When a contract ends, client data is returned or securely deleted within 30 days, and a signed certificate of deletion is provided.

## Data Classification
All data is classified into four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Personal data and financial data are always treated as Restricted.

## Breach Notification
In the event of a personal data breach, we notify the affected client within 24 hours of confirming the breach, so the client can meet its own regulatory deadlines.

## Use of AI on Client Data
Client data is never used to train AI models without the client's explicit written consent.
