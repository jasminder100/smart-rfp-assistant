## About Us
NimbusEdge Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is an IT services company founded in 2014 and headquartered in Bengaluru, India, with delivery centres in Pune and Hyderabad and a sales office in Dubai, UAE. We employ approximately 1,850 engineers and consultants.

## Core Service Lines
1. Cloud Migration and Modernization (AWS, Azure, GCP)
2. DevOps, Platform Engineering and SRE
3. Data Engineering and Analytics
4. Managed Cloud Operations (24x7)
5. Application Development and Maintenance
