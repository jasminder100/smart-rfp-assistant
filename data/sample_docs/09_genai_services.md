# NimbusEdge Technologies — Generative AI Services

**Document type:** Service offering section (v1.0, 2026)

## GenAI Offerings
NimbusEdge builds Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) assistants, document
summarisation tools and AI chatbots for enterprises. We work with Amazon Bedrock,
Azure OpenAI and open-source models.

## Responsible AI
Every GenAI solution includes guardrails, human review for client-facing output,
and source citations. Client data is never used to train models without consent.

## Experience
Since 2024 we have delivered 12 GenAI projects, including an RFP response
assistant for a Dubai-based IT consultancy that cut proposal drafting time by 60%.
